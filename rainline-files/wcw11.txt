THE LAST ISSUE
by packetfox

Our next-site link is broken again. Last month the site after ours had
moved from the university to somebody's spare 486. This month the person
with the password moved. If you can still get to the guestbook, leave a
note with the new address. Old bookmarks are half our directory by now.

A MIRROR IN THE BASEMENT

The local Linux mirror moved off the box that runs the board. The sysop
says this will help the phone line. The other benefit is that a bad CGI
script can take down the counter without taking down everybody's mail.

If you keep a mirror, keep the boring bits too. A 404 page might explain
where a project went. A guestbook might have the only surviving copy of
the new address. Keep the directory names when you burn the CD.

SIGNING OFF

Thanks to the folks who read the text edition in Lynx, sent us fixes
over the echo, or called just long enough to grab the issue and clear
the line. We're leaving the back issues up for as long as the disk lasts.
